About:

AURA explores a core barrier to AI in finance: people want smarter financial help, but they don’t fully trust AI with their money. The product is designed to build that trust through explainable recommendations, explicit approvals, and progressive autonomy across cash, investments, and crypto.

01The problem

AI can understand your money. But would you let it move it?

User problem

Users want smarter financial help, but don’t fully trust AI to act with their money.

Business problem

AI insights often stop at awareness: recommendations don’t turn into actions, automation adoption stays low, and recurring AI value remains weak.

Core tension

More autonomy → more convenience, but potentially less trust.

Business objective

Move users from passive financial monitoring toward recurring AI-assisted actions.

How might we

How might we increase the value and usage of AI-powered financial actions without reducing users’ sense of control?

The opportunity wasn't simply to add AI. It was to design the right level of autonomy.

04Insights & hypotheses

Four insights, five testable bets

01

A reason, not just a recommendation

“€1,240 above your buffer; €3,000 stays for emergencies” beats “Invest €700”.

02

Trust depends on the action

Savings ≠ investment ≠ crypto. Savings → automatic, crypto → always ask.

03

AI needs to be proactive

Users don't always know what to ask — so the default is an opportunities feed, not a blank chatbot.

04

Automation should be earned

Suggest → repeated approvals → “Automate transfers up to €200?” Never at onboarding.

10Constraints, outcome & learnings

From an AI chatbot to a controlled financial agent

Constraints

Trust vs automation

More automation adds convenience but can reduce perceived control.

Explainability vs load

Every detail builds transparency but overwhelms simple actions.

Different risk levels

A €20 subscription and a €2,000 crypto trade can't share one approval model.

AI uncertainty

Not deterministic truth: reasoning, editable actions, explicit confirmation.

Scope. I deliberately limited v1 to cash management, investments and crypto — not loans, credit, tax or insurance.

Outcome

Proactive

Discovers opportunities instead of waiting for prompts.

Explainable

Users see why before committing.

Controllable

Users decide what AI can suggest, prepare, automate.

What I learned

Trust is not a single product metric.

Users trust AI to analyse their finances long before they trust it to move money.

Automation should be progressive.

Repeated approvals are a stronger moment than broad permissions upfront.

AI should have one mental model.

One contextual workspace removed duplicated flows and clarified what AURA is.
